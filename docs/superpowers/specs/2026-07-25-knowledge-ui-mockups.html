Refunds & cancellations

docs.acmebilling.com/billing/refunds·docs · public·last crawled 2 h ago

You can cancel your Acme Billing subscription at any time from Settings → Plan. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period — you keep full access until then.

Refunds for annual plans

Annual plans canceled within 30 days of purchase are refunded in full, automatically, to the original payment method. After 30 days we refund the unused months on a pro-rata basis when you contact support.

Refunds for monthly plans

Monthly charges are non-refundable once the billing period has started, with two exceptions:

  • You were charged after canceling — refunded in full within 3 business days.
  • A duplicate charge on the same invoice — the duplicate is voided immediately.

Mid-cycle cancellations

If you cancel mid-cycle we do not charge again, and any metered overage already accrued is billed on the final invoice. Seats removed mid-cycle are credited to that invoice.

You can pause your subscription instead of canceling. Pausing keeps your account, data, and settings intact while billing stops.

How to pause

  1. Go to Settings → Plan and choose “Pause subscription”.
  2. Pick a duration — 1, 2, or 3 months. We won't invoice you during the pause.
  3. Your subscription resumes automatically at the end of the pause, or earlier if you press “Resume” any time.

What pausing does not do

  • Metered usage already accrued this cycle is still billed on your next invoice.
  • API keys stay active in read-only mode; write calls return 402 while paused.

invoice object

docs/api/invoice.md·api · public·pushed 41 min ago · main @ 4c1f2ea

An invoice represents one billing event for a customer — subscription renewals, seat changes, and metered overage all land on invoices.

Fields

  • id — inv_…, unique per invoice.
  • status — draft → open → paid, or void / uncollectible.
  • amount_due — integer, minor units (cents). Never a float.
  • lines[] — line items; each carries a seat count or a metered quantity, never both.
  • attempt_count — dunning attempts so far; capped at 4 before the invoice is marked uncollectible.

Can I get a refund if I cancel mid-cycle?

Yes — it depends on your plan. On a monthly plan, canceling mid-cycle stops future charges but the current month isn't refunded; any metered overage you've already used is billed on your final invoice, and seats you remove are credited to it.

On an annual plan, you get a full automatic refund within 30 days of purchase, and a pro-rata refund of unused months after that if you contact support.
