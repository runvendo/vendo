```text auth/act-as
warning: actAs is configured and declined the doctor's synthetic principal
(<detail>) — expected when a subject→user resolver only mints for real
users; real away runs depend on it accepting real subjects
```

`<detail>` is the wire's own decline reason, or `no detail` when it didn't
carry one.

`check: auth/act-as` · `error_code: E-AUTH-008` · `doctor exits: 0`

## What you're seeing

A warning: the `actAs` seam is wired and answered, with a no.

## Why

This is expected when your `subject → user` resolver only mints for
subjects in your host's real user table; doctor's synthetic test subject is
not one of them. The doctor line names the wire's own decline reason.

## The fix

If the decline is your resolver correctly refusing an unknown subject,
there is nothing to fix; that is the fail-closed behavior working as
intended. If the reason instead names a missing secret, set it
(`AUTH_SECRET`, or the preset's documented variable) and re-run
`npx vendo doctor`.
