## What your agent will do

- Read `package.json` and detect your framework, package manager, and auth.
- Run `npx vendo init`, which does everything deterministic itself.
- Relay init's questions to you — how people will use your agent, where
  screens live — instead of guessing.
- If your repo already runs its own agent loop, stop and ask whether to keep
  it and add Vendo's tools instead of installing the full agent.
- Paste the one snippet init prints, then verify the wiring with
  `vendo doctor`.

You stay in the loop for every question and every account. The agent does the
typing.

## The playbook itself

Agents that land here follow the [install playbook](/agents) — the raw,
machine-readable version at `docs.vendo.run/agents.md`. It's written for
them, not for you.
